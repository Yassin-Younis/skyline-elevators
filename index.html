L02 Elevator Types

A guide to vertical transportation

From glass panoramic cars to compact home lifts and heavy-duty service elevators, every SKYLINE system is engineered for its building and finished to the client's request.

Panoramic Elevators

A revolutionary concept in elevator design.

Panorama elevators offer a unique and innovative experience for riders. Walls and ceilings made entirely of glass provide a stunning 360-degree view of the surrounding area.

They redefine the way people perceive vertical transportation, providing breathtaking views and a sense of openness during the ride. Ideal for atriums, malls, hotels and signature residences.

Machine-Room Elevators

Experience and enjoy the quiet, efficient lift of MR elevators.

Machine-Room (MR) elevators are known for their quiet operation, durability and energy-saving capabilities. Commonly used in taller buildings, they employ traction systems with ropes and counterweights.

They incorporate advanced safety features and technology such as emergency braking systems and smart monitoring, with well-ventilated machine rooms to dissipate heat.

Machine-Room-Less (MRL)

The future of vertical transportation.

MRL elevators are a cost-effective, space-saving solution designed to eliminate the need for a separate machine room, freeing valuable floor area in the building.

Compact gearless traction systems power the cabin for smooth, quiet rides, backed by emergency braking and redundant safety systems. A popular choice for residential and commercial buildings alike.

Home Lifts

The convenient solution for multi-level living.

Home lifts greatly improve accessibility and convenience in a multi-storey home, and are especially beneficial for elderly or mobility-impaired residents.

Each lift is customised to fit the style and layout of the specific home, blending seamlessly with the interior while offering a practical, stylish solution for multi-storey living.

L08 FAQ

Questions, answered

Quick answers to common enquiries. If you need anything else, our knowledgeable team is here to help.

Ask our team
01What types of elevators do you offer?
We offer a wide range of elevators, including passenger elevators, freight elevators, home elevators, panoramic elevators and more. Our elevators are designed to meet various needs and requirements.
02Do you provide annual maintenance contracts?
Yes. Our comprehensive yearly maintenance contracts ensure regular inspections, preventative maintenance and prompt support, allowing for smooth and reliable elevator operation throughout the year.
03Are your elevators compliant with safety standards and regulations?
Absolutely. Our company is certified in civil defence and our elevators are manufactured to meet or exceed industry safety standards and comply with all relevant regulations. Safety is a core aspect of our elevator solutions.
04Can you customise elevators to suit specific building requirements?
Yes, we specialise in tailored elevator solutions. Our team works closely with clients to understand their unique needs and customise elevator designs accordingly, ensuring seamless integration with the building structure.
05What is the typical lead time for supply and installation?
Lead time varies with the scope of the project, building requirements and elevator type. Normally supply takes 60 to 100 days and installation 7 to 15 days. Our team will assess your specific needs and provide a detailed timeline during consultation.
06Do you offer after-sales support and warranty?
Yes. We provide 2 years of comprehensive after-sales support and warranty coverage for our elevators. Our customer service team is available to assist with any queries or concerns even after installation is complete.
07How can I request a quote or schedule a consultation?
Simply fill out the contact form below or call us on the unified number 9200 28880. Our sales representatives will promptly assist you and guide you through the next steps.
08What measures ensure sustainable, eco-friendly solutions?
We incorporate energy-efficient technologies such as LED lighting and regenerative drives to minimise environmental impact, and prioritise recyclable and eco-friendly materials in our manufacturing processes.
09Do you offer 24/7 emergency support for breakdowns?
Absolutely. We provide round-the-clock emergency support to address any unexpected breakdowns or malfunctions promptly, minimising downtime.
